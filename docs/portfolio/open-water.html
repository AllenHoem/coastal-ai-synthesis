P1

Document Intake & Exception Handling

"How do we process invoices, claims, and orders without hiring — and without silent errors?" ▸

Ingesting, validating, and posting structured documents does not need inference at the front door. Typed validation and business rules absorb the majority of volume deterministically; a language model is invoked only on exceptions, and escalates through a ladder of increasing capability — local small model, sampled consensus, cloud field-level fix, cloud full-document — with an independent verification gate at every rung.

Pillar B in detail

AI @ the Edge: measure, don't guess

The edge pitch is simple: most enterprise AI workloads don't need a frontier model, and many aren't allowed to use one. The hard question is which model is small enough to be cheap and big enough to be safe — and that is a measurement question. Our answer is a harness, not an opinion: find the smallest model that clears the silent-failure threshold for the client's workload, and that determines the hardware tier they need.

REFRAMED — right instinct, wrong mechanism

4 · "Without machine learning, inference alone will always risk a degree of hallucination"

What the evidence says: hallucination is intrinsic to LLM inference, and classical ML does not remove it. What actually reduces error in production systems is the compound-systems toolkit: retrieval grounding, independent verification gates, best-of-N reranking with factuality scoring (shown in ACL 2025 findings to significantly lower error rates), and evaluation harnesses run continuously. Classical ML is critical — but as the system's numerical judgment, not as a hallucination cure.

Where ML is genuinely load-bearing (the examples you asked for):

  • Learned routers & cascades — a small classifier decides which model (or no model) gets each request; FrugalGPT-style routing cuts cost 60–90% at equal quality.
  • Rerankers — cross-encoders ordering retrieved context; the single highest-leverage accuracy upgrade in RAG systems.
  • Propensity & uplift models — the "weights" in next-best-action; the LLM explains, the ML model scores.
  • Confidence calibration — mapping model logprobs to real error rates so escalation triggers fire at the right threshold (noting grammar-constrained outputs are systematically over-confident and must be segmented).
  • Anomaly & fraud detection — gating agent autonomy: the agent drafts, the anomaly model decides whether a human sees it first.
  • Forecasting — demand/pipeline forecasts as structured context for agent planning.
  • Entity resolution & dedupe — embedding-based matching that fixes the data before any reasoning happens.
  • Drift monitors — distribution-shift detectors that page a human before quality decays, not after.
Portfolio consequence: this becomes Pillar D / Pattern P6 — "Evaluation, ML & AI Operations" — positioned as the accuracy-and-assurance layer under every other pattern, rather than an anti-hallucination claim we can't defend to a technical buyer.

The AI & Platform Delivery team

Day-1 structure follows the AI & Platform Delivery framework: 11 seats — a leadership spine of five plus six forward-deployed engineers (FDEs) in two teams under two Sr. Directors, delivering in partnership with the markets. The split is the strategy: Salesforce-side capacity is deliberately capped so the alliance stays healthy without re-centering the practice on one vendor, while the agnostic bench is where net-new capability compounds. Roughly 8 of 11 seats rebadge from the existing bench — three external fills at day-1; the ~9 specialist profiles in the SME map below are the FY28 at-scale need, not the day-1 ask. proposed

Salesforce channel friction

"Lead agnostic" can be read in San Francisco as defection, chilling co-sell.

Mitigation: P3 keeps Agentforce as the named best-fit chassis; the Claude-in-trust-boundary story lets us be agnostic inside Salesforce's own narrative. Brief the alliance team before any public launch.

TCS overlap

TCS has its own AI practices; a Coastal AI portfolio could look duplicative to the new parent.

Mitigation: position Open Water as TCS's fast, productized execution layer for North America mid-market and regulated accounts — the speedboat that complements TCS's global scale and aligns with TCS's own Anthropic partnership. Get ELT + TCS sponsor alignment in the Q1 launch gate.

Specialist talent

Edge inference and applied-ML engineers are the market's scarcest profiles — 3 external fills at day-1, roughly 9 by FY28 scale.

Mitigation: the Edge Lab + published measurement work is the recruiting magnet; the Academy pipeline caps external dependence; TCS global bench as surge capacity.

Becoming the 40%

Gartner's canceled-projects prediction applies to our clients' engagements too — a failed pilot cohort would kill the launch story.

Mitigation: the Pathfinder stage is a real go/no-go gate with measured accuracy thresholds; we decline pilots the evidence doesn't support. Saying no is the brand.
