Pillar C in detail

Patterns, not platforms

Each pattern is named for the question it answers. P1

Document Intake & Exception Handling

"How do we process invoices, claims, and orders without hiring — and without silent errors?" ▸

Ingesting, validating, and posting structured documents does not need inference at the front door. Typed validation and business rules absorb the majority of volume deterministically; a language model is invoked only on exceptions, and escalates through a ladder of increasing capability — local small model, sampled consensus, cloud field-level fix, cloud full-document — with an independent verification gate at every rung.

Deterministic tierTyped checks, arithmetic rules, schema validation — no model call
→
R0 · Local SLMGrammar-constrained extraction, temp 0, on-prem
→
R1 · Consensusk=3 sampled, majority vote, still local
→
R2 · Cloud fieldEscalate only the failing field to a frontier model
→
R3 · Cloud docFull-document frontier pass — last resort
→
Verifier gateIndependent of the model. Gate decision and correctness are never collapsed

The headline metric is the silent-failure rate — documents the gate passed with wrong values. It is reported standalone, never averaged into success, because it is the number a CFO actually fears.

KPIs: straight-through %, silent-failure rate (<2% threshold), cost per 1,000 documents, docs/hour/machine

Pillar B in detail

AI @ the Edge: measure, don't guess

The edge pitch is simple: most enterprise AI workloads don't need a frontier model, and many aren't allowed to use one. The hard question is which model is small enough to be cheap and big enough to be safe — and that is a measurement question. Our answer is a harness, not an opinion: find the smallest model that clears the silent-failure threshold for the client's workload, and that determines the hardware tier they need.

The measurement harness behind the practice

Coastal AI Synthesis — our internal edge-first measurement harness — runs a client-representative corpus through every model size and hardware tier and reports three things per configuration: silent-failure rate, throughput per machine, and cost per 1,000 records, with every number badged measured, modeled, or projected. It is the engine inside the Edge AI Pathfinder offer: clients get an evidence file, not a slideware recommendation. Architecture is complete; first measured runs are the Q1 milestone on the roadmap.

Salesforce channel friction

"Lead agnostic" can be read in San Francisco as defection, chilling co-sell.

Mitigation: P3 keeps Agentforce as the named best-fit chassis; the Claude-in-trust-boundary story lets us be agnostic inside Salesforce's own narrative. Brief the alliance team before any public launch.

TCS overlap

TCS has its own AI practices; a Coastal AI portfolio could look duplicative to the new parent.

Mitigation: position Open Water as TCS's fast, productized execution layer for North America mid-market and regulated accounts — the speedboat that complements TCS's global scale and aligns with TCS's own Anthropic partnership. Get ELT + TCS sponsor alignment in the Q1 launch gate.

Specialist talent

Edge inference and applied-ML engineers are the market's scarcest profiles — 3 external fills at day-1, roughly 9 by FY28 scale.

Mitigation: the Edge Lab + published measurement work is the recruiting magnet; the Academy pipeline caps external dependence; TCS global bench as surge capacity.

Becoming the 40%

Gartner's canceled-projects prediction applies to our clients' engagements too — a failed pilot cohort would kill the launch story.

Mitigation: the Pathfinder stage is a real go/no-go gate with measured accuracy thresholds; we decline pilots the evidence doesn't support. Saying no is the brand.
